Privacy: this application requests microphone permission only. It has no Internet
permission, analytics, background capture service, contacts access, clipboard
history, or automatic recording/transcript storage. Capture starts when you tap
Speak and ends on Stop, cancellation, leaving the voice view, or the visible
120-second limit. Recordings are processed in memory. Preview text expires after
two minutes and is discarded when leaving or changing fields. Memory cleanup is
best effort; it is not a forensic erasure guarantee.

Model import uses the Android document picker and stores a verified copy in the
private no-backup directory. Backup and device transfer are disabled. Deleting
the imported model does not delete the original file in Downloads. Opening the
download link sends you to your browser and requires a confirmation.

IME insertion sends text to the current app. Speech-intent results go to the
calling app after you tap Insert. That app and your selected keyboard have their
own privacy policies. Utterleaf cannot control their retention. Our IME avoids
password fields but a speech-intent caller controls its own destination.
